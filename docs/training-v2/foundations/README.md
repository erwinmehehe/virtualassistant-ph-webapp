## What this pack does, and does not, do

- Defines a proposed **34-hour-30-minute nominal learning path**. These are planning estimates including practice; individual learners may need more or less time, and hours are not proof of competence.
- Sets a higher instructional bar than reading plus a short written response.
- Demonstrates that bar through one detailed lesson learners can complete with fictional data and free spreadsheet tools.
- Includes both teaching practice and a distinct rubric for reviewing the resulting work. The current production app does **not** automatically grade these new files or deliver a V2 credential.
- Contains **no migrations, app routes, certificate changes, feature toggles, email sends, account changes, or live deployment**. No training data is changed by this branch.

## Important safety / transition rules

The repository's 14 October audit describes the published **Virtual Assistant Foundations** as having 10 consolidated lessons. Preserve its identifiers, published content, learner progress, existing assessment results and issued credentials.

If V2 is approved, build it as a versioned curriculum with a distinct enrollment, progress and assessment identity. Never automatically substitute V2 learning evidence for existing training completions. Offer a clear migration or optional bridge path only after product and learner-consent review.

Training exercises and the pilot dataset are entirely fictional. Do not submit real client contacts, financial records, patient details, credentials or work products into public learning files or an unapproved AI model.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] VA trainer and employer validate task realism and boundaries.
- [ ] Instructional designer tests pilot lesson with novice learners.
- [ ] Accessibility reviewer approves keyboard, small-screen, screen-reader and downloadable formats.
- [ ] Software trainer approves any branded product walkthroughs against a test account and current UI.
- [ ] Human assessor trials the rubric and gives actionable feedback.
- [ ] Product/engineering specify practical upload, autosave, reviews and appeal/redo support.
- [ ] Compliance review approves privacy, IP, certificate language and software-specific regulated boundaries.
- [ ] Only then: create a separate implementation plan and feature-controlled staging release.

**Do not merge this documentation PR as an instruction to publish V2.** It is a reviewable curriculum specification and example, not a release.
