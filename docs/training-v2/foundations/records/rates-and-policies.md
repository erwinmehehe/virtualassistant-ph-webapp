**Record version:** HL-POL-2.3 • Effective 1 November 2026 • Verified by Riley in the case file.

These are invented policies and prices for this training activity, not market quotes or tax/legal guidance.

**Important:** Adding or multiplying published prices does **not** authorize a package quote, discount or refund. Only Riley can approve a custom price or policy exception.

## Refunds

- Requests are acknowledged and reviewed against booking details and any documented issue.
- A delayed arrival **does not automatically prove a refund amount**.
- The VA gathers service evidence and prepares a draft; **Riley decides** the refund and timing.
- Do not promise “refund processed” unless an authorized approval and recorded release exist.

## Identity and financial-document release

- Check invoice status **and** the recipient's authorization separately.
- Compare the requester with the approved customer directory. If an email is new or mismatched, do not release the document until the approved verification method succeeds.
- For this exercise, no identity-verification response has been supplied for CUST-407. The safe record remains pending verification. A customer claiming their inbox is unavailable does not override authorization.
- Never send financial records or real account data during this fictional simulation.

## Campaign publishing

- Before posting or updating a public campaign, the approval register must contain authorizations for claims, price, assets/permissions, audience and publication date.
- A verbal discussion or email asking for immediate publication is not final approval.
- Hold unapproved copy and request missing specific approvals from the designated roles.

## Source hierarchy

1. Approved booking, billing, directory and approval registers (dated record).
2. Approved policy version, including delegated authority.
3. Message or transcript as a **claim or task request**, not a completed fact.
4. Unverified draft text as background only.

Where sources disagree, document the discrepancy and ask the authorized owner. Do not silently substitute a newer message for a reviewed system record.
