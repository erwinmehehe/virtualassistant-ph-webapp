What this checker can and cannot do
        
The checker flags empty required fields only. It cannot validate that your priority, evidence, dates, recipient or action is correct. A trained reviewer must assess those decisions against the provided fictional source documents and the public rubric. The Export button includes incomplete records so you can resume later.
