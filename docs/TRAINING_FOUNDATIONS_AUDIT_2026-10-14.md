# VAPH Foundations curriculum QA — 2026-10-14

The production Training Hub has 26 published courses and 264 published of 267 stored lessons. The three unpublished lessons are in Virtual Assistant Foundations, where ten consolidated lessons are already published.

## Decision

Do **not** publish the remaining three lessons as additional required lessons. The equivalent content is already incorporated into the ten public lessons, with practical exercises, reusable templates, and QA checklists. Publishing the old split drafts would duplicate instruction and retroactively change required completion counts for existing learners and certificate holders.

| Unpublished older lesson | Existing consolidated published lesson |
| --- | --- |
| Professional Standards, Boundaries, and Confidentiality | The VA Role, Standards, Boundaries, and Confidentiality |
| Updates, Questions, Mistakes, and Escalation | Client Communication, Updates, Mistakes, and Escalation |
| Time Zones, Deadlines, and Handoffs | Priorities, Deadlines, Time Zones, and Handoffs |

## Follow-through

- Keep these three former split lessons hidden in the database for historical reference.
- Retain the ten published Foundations lessons and existing final assessment/certificate requirements.
- Improve new-enrolment routing into the first published lesson.
- Show clear progress sync errors instead of silently losing reading engagement.
- Use the admin-only course funnel, mature cohorts, next-lesson bottlenecks and activation report to measure learner progress without automated outreach or exposing personal details.
- Confirm an authenticated learner can start Foundations, save progress, return to the correct lesson, complete practice/checkpoint requirements, and pass a final assessment in a non-production test.

This document records a content mapping decision, not a production deployment or certification claim.
